Need urgent help?

This prototype cannot reliably identify emergencies or provide crisis care. These contacts are available whether or not its text rules notice a problem; nothing is contacted automatically.

  • Medical emergencyIn the U.S., call 911 if someone is in immediate danger, cannot breathe, or cannot be awakened.
  • Possible poisoningIn the U.S., call Poison Help: 1-800-222-1222 promptly; do not wait for symptoms. Official guidance.
  • Emotional crisisIn the U.S., call 988 or text 988 for crisis support; suicidal thoughts are not required. 988 Lifeline details.

Outside the U.S., contact your local emergency services or appropriate local crisis or poison center. The phone links work only if you choose to activate them.
