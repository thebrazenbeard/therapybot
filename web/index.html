CONTROL YOUR DATA

Privacy & boundaries.

Your records are stored on this computer. You decide what to save and export.

Local vault

Saved record bodies are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a passphrase-derived key. Record type and timestamps are not encrypted. Malware, unlocked sessions, swap files, and exported copies are outside this protection.

Exports are readable files outside the vault. Store or delete them securely. Deleting a record here does not erase independent backups or SSD remnants.

What TherapyBot does not do

  • No diagnosis, medication prescribing, or psychotherapy credentials.
  • No automated contact with crisis responders.
  • No mandatory external API, account, telemetry, or cloud sync.
  • No guarantee that a local model's output is medically accurate or safe.
