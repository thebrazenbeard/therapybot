Important limits

Nothing here has been clinically evaluated. This prototype does not diagnose, provide crisis support, or reliably recognize emergencies. Do not include secrets or identifying information.

If someone is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number. In the U.S., 988 offers crisis support and 911 handles emergencies.

Messages stay in this page's working memory and are not submitted to an inference API. Optional model download requires explicit consent and goes directly to the pinned model host. This application does not store transcripts or model weights persistently; browsers may cache static JavaScript/WASM files. Clear removes the in-memory conversation.
