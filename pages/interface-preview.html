THE APPROACH

Built around boundaries that matter.

Thoughtful conversation does not require an account, a subscription, or giving your private history to an online service.

01 / LOCAL
⌂

Your machine is the host.

The application runs on Windows or Linux with a separately configured local GGUF-compatible model endpoint. No mandatory cloud inference or paid API.

02 / CHOICE
◌

Nothing saved by default.

Choose whether to keep a conversation. Optional encrypted local records and journaling use a passphrase; privacy still depends on the computer and model runtime.

03 / INQUIRY
⌁

Questions stay questions.

Explore relationships, behavior, identity, trauma and philosophy without treating every unusual idea as a symptom or every reflection as a diagnosis.

EVIDENCE, WITH LIMITS

A source is a starting point, not a verdict.

TherapyBot's local reference library uses traceable summaries and links covering psychological methods, mental health and social context. It is deliberately open to scrutiny.

A

Psychology & behavior

CBT-informed reflection, ACT values work, DBT skills, emotional regulation and trauma-aware conversation.

B

Social context

Relationships, isolation, adversity and social determinants—not just what happens inside one person's head.

C

Uncertainty & philosophy

Identity, spirituality, consciousness and competing explanations, with a distinction between inquiry and established evidence.

Public sources are not automatically licensed for model training. The linked research register is metadata and original short summaries; no outside article full texts or private personal records are included by default.

    Start a local model

    Configure KoboldCPP, llama.cpp or a compatible loopback model endpoint.

    Open your own interface

    Launch TherapyBot, then visit its local browser address. Saving records remains your choice.
