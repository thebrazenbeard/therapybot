Local records

The vault stores journal entries and exchanges you explicitly choose to save. A passphrase protects the stored records; losing it may make existing data unrecoverable.

Opening this page does not create a cloud account. Your records stay in the local application storage. Security also depends on your computer and the configured runtime.

Data controls

Download an encrypted local database backup, or permanently remove all saved conversation and journal records. Keep your backup and passphrase in a safe place.

Deleting records is irreversible in this application. A separate downloaded backup will not be removed.
